Solution 1: Decentralized Urban Composting Hubs

To directly address the high volume of organic waste, Team 24 proposes an automated, community-scale composting model that transforms food waste into vital agricultural resources.

What It Does

This system intercepts organic and biodegradable waste before it enters municipal collection. By rapidly decomposing food waste at the community level, it produces high-yield, nutrient-rich compost that can be used for local urban agriculture, directly supporting Bangkok's access to affordable and nutritious food while eliminating methane emissions from rotting landfill waste.

How It Works

Using aerobic decomposition chambers equipped with passive aeration and moisture balancing, the model accelerates the breakdown of organics. It features a sealed, pest-proof entry port and a rotational drum mechanism that users or community caretakers can easily operate to mix the compost and maintain optimal oxygen levels.

⚠️ System Downside & Maintenance Constraints

The primary downside is odor and pest management if the carbon-to-nitrogen ratio is not perfectly maintained by the community. Furthermore, it requires strict behavioral compliance; if non-organic waste (like plastic) contaminates the composting chamber, the resulting fertilizer will be unusable for food crops.

Solution 2: Closed-Loop Recycling & Landfill Diversion

To aggressively tackle Bangkok's landfill dependency and prevent ecological leakage, we propose a localized, closed-loop dry waste sorting and compaction model.

What It Does

This infrastructure physically prevents plastic, glass, and metal from reaching open landfills or waterways. By sorting and compacting dry recyclables at the source, it prepares materials for direct sale to recycling plants. This drastically reduces the total mass sent to landfills and protects local Natural Habitats from plastic suffocation and microplastic leakage.

How It Works

The system utilizes standardized, color-coded drop-off points linked to a localized compaction press. Once materials are deposited, a mechanical press reduces the volume of plastics and aluminum, maximizing storage efficiency until collection. This acts as a hyper-local transfer station, bridging the gap between household waste generation and formal recycling facilities.

⚠️ System Downside & Maintenance Constraints

The major constraint is the initial manufacturing and installation cost of the sorting and compaction units. Additionally, the system relies heavily on users pre-washing their plastics; heavily soiled food containers can contaminate the entire batch, attract pests, and ultimately reduce the economic value of the recycled materials.
